PASS if the answer says the conditions precedent to the initial loan (the initial Credit Extension on the Closing
Date) are in Section 4.01 (Conditions to All Credit Extensions) and Section 4.02 (Additional Conditions to Initial
Funding on the Closing Date), citing both section numbers, and summarises several of the conditions themselves.

FAIL if it omits Section 4.01 or Section 4.02, if it presents Section 4.03 (conditions to each subsequent Credit
Extension) as a condition to the initial loan, or if it says it could not read the document.
