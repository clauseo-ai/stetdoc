# stetdoc docs

Scope: site/app/docs/**, every page under stetdoc.clauseo.chat/docs, and the site's 404 (app/not-found.tsx). Mode: **Read**. Visitor: a developer setting stetdoc up for their coding agent, an agent told to install it, or a product builder embedding it; many arrive on a phone from a shared link. Success: they find the page, read it without friction, copy the command, and move on. Proof: the repo's own markdown, rendered as is, with commands whose output is real. Constraints: the launch page's world (Clauseo tokens through custom properties only, Anek, ink marks, no hue for meaning, planes not lines), static export, zero framework JS, our own inline JS at most 4 KB gzipped, every touch control 44 px or more.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The launch page's world, with its voice turned down to reading. The article sits on the bare concrete; the chrome is quiet type; depth only where something is set in (code wells) or raised (callouts, doors, pager, sheets, dock). The stet mark appears in four places and does a job in each: the header lockup, the current-page bar in the sidebar and TOC, the three dots that end every page, and the full mark over "Stet." on the 404, its one loud moment. It refuses grey panel columns, outlined cards, a syntax palette and a floating chat widget.

OWN-WORLD: Same tokens as the launch page (ground, well, surface, inset; ink, body, muted, quiet; divide, line), read only through var(). Anek carries every voice of the chrome and prose: 125% for the H1, 116% for heads and labels, 104% for reading, 90% for inline code. Code wells are set in (--set-in) at 10 px; callouts, doors and pager cards are flat surface planes (--shadow-flat); sheets and the floating dock are raised (--e2). Hairlines are 1 px inset shadows.

## What was built, and why

- **Navigation, one form per band** (the table below). On S and M a dock sits in the thumb zone: Menu, On this page (only with three or more TOC entries), Search. On S it is a full-width ground plane with an upward shade; on M it floats as a raised 480 px plane. Each button opens an HTML popover sheet (popover/popovertarget): they open, close, take Esc and light dismiss with JavaScript off. Close sits at the bottom of each sheet, under the thumb. From L a sticky header with the search key and a sticky sidebar take over and the dock goes. On L the header also carries an "On this page" key that opens the same TOC sheet, hung under the key; from XL a sticky "On this page" column replaces the key. "On this page" is offered once at every size: the old details block under the H1 is gone, because on phones and tablets it repeated the dock's button and cost 48 to 60 px of the first screen. On a phone turned sideways (SHORT) nothing sticks over the text: the header scrolls away, the dock's three keys sit in it, and the sheets come in from the side with Close in their title row. iOS Safari 26's floating toolbar was **not** checked on a device: that is the open risk, and SHORT already shows the fallback (the dock in the header row).
- **Search.** Pagefind, our own UI. pagefind.js loads by dynamic import() on the first open or the first shortcut, never before (network log: only the Anek woff2 loads with the page). "/" and Cmd K or Ctrl K open it; Enter in the field opens the first result, the arrows move focus through the result links and back up to the field, typing returns to the field, Esc closes and focus returns to the opener. (A combobox with aria-activedescendant was built first; html-validate's prefer-native-element rule rejects role=combobox and role=listbox in check-docs, so the results are a plain list of links with roving focus.) Full screen on S with the field at the top, 17 px, enterkeyhint=search; a panel at 10 dvh on M; from L the panel opens over the header's search key, its right edge on the key's, so it grows out of the field that opened it. Results show the page title, the section and the matched heading, and a two-line excerpt with the match in ink weight, never colour. With JS off, `@media (scripting: none)` swaps the field for "Search needs JavaScript, which is off in this browser. Every page is in the menu." and a button that opens the menu sheet.
- **Page actions.** Three small keys, flat surface planes. On M, L and XL they sit under the H1 with their labels. On S and SHORT they keep only their icons, carry their names for screen readers, and move up beside the breadcrumbs: three 44 px keys in one 148 px row, so the H1 and the lead start a row sooner. Icon keys won over a single "Page" menu because every action stays one tap, and Copy page, the one most readers come for, isn't folded away. Copy page copies the twin's exact text from a `<template>`, or for pages over 60 KB fetches the twin inside a ClipboardItem promise so Safari keeps the click's permission. View as markdown is a plain link. Open in is a popover of **five** AI apps (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Le Chat, Perplexity) in alphabetical order, from one array in strings.ts, each with its monochrome mark at the same size and no favourite. Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini are left out: Copilot's `?q=` is dropped by a redirect to its home page, and Gemini has no URL that prefills a prompt, so a link to either would open an empty chat. The menu hangs under its key where CSS anchor positioning exists, is a bottom sheet on S and elsewhere, and on SHORT a panel at the top right with the apps in two columns; it is never taller than the screen. With JS off, Copy page is hidden by `@media (scripting: none)` rather than left dead, and so are the code blocks' Copy buttons.
- **Page foot and end.** Updated date in `<time>` only when git knows it, the honest edit link (doc.edit.label), "Something wrong on this page? Open an issue", then the end mark (the stet's three dots at 22 px) and prev/next cards with the section named.
- **Reading type (plan 6.2).** H1 30 to 34 px across S, 38 to 44 across M, 44 on L and XL, 48 on XXL, 125%, 620, and never over 12% of a SHORT screen. Lead 19/29 → 21/32 from M → 22/34 on XXL, 110%, muted. H2 24/30 → 28/34, H3 19/26 → 21/28, H4 17/26 → 18/28. Body 17/27 at 100% on S, 18/29 at 104% from M, 19/31 on XXL, in --body. Prose measure 31em, about 68 characters a line: counted glyph by glyph in WebKit on the quickstart and the glossary, 32em gave 70 to 71 on average, so it was set one em tighter. It was 68ch, which Anek's wide "0" turned into 88 to 96 real characters. Reading text wraps greedily (`text-wrap: auto`): WebKit's `pretty` evens out the whole paragraph and left phone paragraphs at 81% of the column. Tables and code fill the track, 760 px on M, up to 880 on L and 960 on XL, so they may run wider than prose lines. Headings link to themselves across their full width; a "#" shows in the margin on hover and focus on wide screens only.
- **Components.** Tables: hairline rows, the header on a --line rule; from L every two-column table fixes its first column at 26%, so meanings line up down the CLI page. On S a two-column table becomes pairs between hairlines, the first cell in ink with the second under it and no label repeated (the CLI page went from 146 px a row to about 95 to 130); three columns or more become cards in the well tone, each cell under its column label (data-label). A row titled by a link is wholly that link. Console and text blocks wrap with a 1.1em hanging indent; source and shell blocks keep each line whole and scroll sideways, so a command to copy never splits at a hyphen, with an edge shade driven by `animation-timeline: scroll(self inline)` that shows only where there is more. `hanging-punctuation` applies to running text only: on code lines Safari hung stetdoc's `{` output 8 px left of its prompt. From M a one-line well sets its command beside its label and Copy in one 44 px row. On S every code well bleeds to the screen edges. Callouts are surface planes; a warning carries the stet bar before its label. Inline code is Anek 90% at 560 on a faint well tint, because the docs' prose is dense with identifiers that are also English words. Key-value lists stack on S; from M the key hangs in an 8.5em column as a float, not a grid, because a value can hold text, a paragraph and a code block, and a grid scattered those across both columns. The overview's three doors are surface planes, each wholly tappable through its first link.
- **404.** The full mark over "Stet." set at the mark's width, "That page isn't here. The docs are." (each sentence held on one line), the search key, a line for a mistyped launch-page address ("Looking for the launch page? Go to stetdoc.clauseo.chat"), and every docs page by section. No sidebar. S and M stack it, the mark capped at 30svh so the words reach the first screen; the page list is one column on S and three balanced CSS columns on M. From L, and on SHORT, the mark and "Stet." stand on the left and the words, search and page list sit beside them, so the first screen says the page is missing even at 320 px tall.

## Round 2 (re-review fixes)

Measured in WebKit through `webkit-shot --eval`; shots in /tmp/bp-review/docs-fix2/final/.

- **One site edge.** The docs header, footer and grid take their insets from `var(--site-edge, --edge-x)`, where --edge-x repeats the launch page's `.launch --edge` band by band (20px; 32 to 48px across M; 48px on L; 48px inside the 1440 frame on XL; inside the growing frame on XXL). The logo sits at the same x on / and /docs at every width (820: 40 and 40, was 40 and 30; 1680: 168 and 168, was 168 and 96). The grid follows the same edge, so the logo, the sidebar and the footer share one left edge. On M the column sits on that edge with the slack on the right, rather than centred.
- **XL and XXL columns.** Sidebar and TOC keep their widths; the track takes the rest, up to 960px (752 at 1680, 800 at 1440 and 1920, 960 at 2560). The TOC follows the track at one gap and ends at the site edge, where the header's GitHub glyph ends (1712 and 1712 at 1920). The header sets its parts on the same columns: the search key starts the reading column, and its panel opens from the key's left edge over the article (532 to 1172 at 1920, TOC from 1472), never over the TOC. The lead's measure is the body's 31em at the body's size, so lead and body end together.
- **SHORT rail.** Under SHORT on S and M, three 44px icon keys (Menu, On this page, Search, each with its name) stand fixed at the bottom right, 12px plus the safe area from the edges, in the 76px the grid keeps clear. A scroll timeline fades them in over the header's 48px and keeps them hidden, untappable and out of Tab order while the header shows; without scroll timelines they stay. They open the same sheets.
- **Flags.** A `--flag` in prose outside code (the CLI's "stdin or --ops") and in the search hint is held in `span.nobr`. Long inline code is cut after each slash (never inside "//") with `<wbr>`, so it breaks only at spaces and slashes.
- **Table title links.** On M, SHORT and any coarse pointer, a row titled by a link is wholly that link, as on S, and rows are 44px.
- **Doors.** From M each door is a full-width row with its title beside its text (a float, like the key-value lists): titles take two lines at 1024 and 1133, where three across gave three.
- **Glossary.** On the glossary page the renderer tags a paragraph that opens with a bold term ending in a full stop as `.gl-entry` (`.term` belongs to the launch page's terminal); from M the term hangs in a 9em column, and the "Avoid:" note starts its own line at every size.
- **Sidebar.** The script starts the sidebar at a section's label, never through one, and the top 20px fades while there is more above.
- **Menus under their keys** (Open in, the L "On this page") drop 6px instead of rising 24px, so they never open with a 32px gap that then closes; the gap settles at 8px.
- **Phones.** The 404 sets each section's pages in two columns on S (the list went from 1164 to 768px at 390). The overview names its section on the key row on S and SHORT, as the trail does elsewhere. Shell and source wells keep 16px on the right on S, so the brew line fits at 320 (320/320, was 321/320).
- **Not done:** a labelled "Copy page" key on S (b1 N4). At 320 it fits only if the breadcrumb goes, and the breadcrumb is what anchors that row.

## The code-font decision (plan 6.3 gate): system monospace for multi-line code

The gate rendered `Il1| 0O 8B 5S 2Z rn m cl d`, quotes, dashes, brackets, a real `stetdoc replace` line and a base token at 14 px, phone width, 3x. In Anek at 92%, **I and l are the same stroke, and 0 and O differ only in width**: `l1Il0O` cannot be read reliably, and refs, hashes and flags are exactly where that matters. The fallback was taken: `ui-monospace, 'SF Mono', SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, 'Liberation Mono', monospace` for multi-line `pre` only, zero bytes downloaded. It also restores the column alignment that real stetdoc output uses (`p42 §4.2  Payment …`). Inline code, commands in prose and the chrome stay in Anek, as the plan says.

Evidence: review/docs/code-font-gate-anek-390-3x.png (failed) and review/docs/code-font-gate-system-mono-390-3x.png (taken).

## Critique (own, after the build) and the fixes it led to

Score 34/40 on Nielsen's ten (0 to 4 each): status 4, match 4, control 3 (an in-page jump from a sheet leaves the sheet open with JS off), consistency 4, error prevention 3, recognition 4, flexibility 4, aesthetic 3, recovery 3, help 2 (no inline help beyond the search hint, by design). Fixes made from the looks, in order of weight:

1. On phones the breadcrumbs, H1 and page actions were gone: globals.css hides the launch page's .doc-head under 740 px. Renamed to .dochead.
2. Anek failed the code glyph gate: multi-line code moved to the system monospace (above).
3. Key-value lists scattered values across two grid columns when a value held a code block: now a float.
4. Every page overflowed by 21 px at 320 px (the header): a narrow-phone header step, checked at 303 px too.
5. The changelog's HTML was 65 KB gzipped, over the 60 KB budget, because its 61 KB of markdown sat in the Copy page template: pages over 60,000 bytes now fetch the twin.
6. Landmarks were duplicated (sidebar and menu sheet both "Docs pages", three TOC navs): the sheets and the inline TOC are no longer landmarks.
7. Search results and the Open in list drew hairlines on rounded rows, which read as broken cards: hairlines now sit on the rows, inset, and hide next to the hovered or focused row.
8. The 404's mark and word were unrelated sizes: "Stet." is now set at the mark's width.
9. The sheet title read as another section label: now a 19 px head over a hairline.

## Decisions to keep

- Docs CSS never names a colour: every tone is a var(); scrims and shades are color-mix() of a token, with a dark-scheme override that mixes from --inset so the scrim stays dark at night.
- globals.css styles .code, .copy, .ln, .cmd, .doc-head, .nav and .sheet for the launch page. The docs use .dnav, .dsheet and .dochead, and scope code rules under `.prose figure.code`. Re-run a class-collision check after any launch-page change.
- The TOC's current-section marker is set by the docs script (a scroll listener, rAF-throttled), not a CSS scroll timeline: a view timeline per heading can't express "the section after this heading until the next one".
- The docs scale (above) is outside DESIGN.md's launch-page ramp on purpose; impeccable detect reports those sizes as advisories.
